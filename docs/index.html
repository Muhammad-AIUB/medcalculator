Privacy Policy

Pocket Medical Calculator · Developed by EXHORT · Last updated: 7 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Pocket Medical Calculator app (the “App”, package name com.exhort.pocketcalculator), developed by EXHORT, handles your information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. The calculators work offline

All clinical calculators in the App run entirely on your device and work fully offline. The values you enter into calculators are processed locally and are never transmitted to us or to any server.

2. Information we collect

The App does not require you to create an account. We only collect personal information if you voluntarily choose to use the optional “Subscribe” form. If you submit it, we collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number
  • Profession and country

Subscribing is entirely optional — you can use every calculator without providing any of this information.

3. How we use your information

If you subscribe, we use your information only to send you updates about the App, new calculators, and clinical tips. We do not sell your information or share it with third parties for advertising.

4. How your information is stored

Submitted subscription details are transmitted securely over HTTPS and stored privately by the developer (in a Google Sheet accessible only to the developer). Reasonable measures are taken to protect this information.

5. Data retention and deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your subscription data at any time by emailing us. We will action verified requests promptly.

6. Children

The App is intended for use by qualified healthcare professionals and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Medical disclaimer

The App is a reference tool for qualified healthcare professionals and does not replace clinical judgement, diagnosis, or treatment decisions.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
